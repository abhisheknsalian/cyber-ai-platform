A botnet is a network of compromised devices controlled remotely by attackers.

Botnets are commonly used for:
- DDoS attacks
- spam campaigns
- credential stuffing
- malware distribution

Common indicators:
- unusual outbound traffic
- communication with command-and-control servers
- coordinated malicious activity

MITRE ATT&CK Technique:
- T1584: Compromise Infrastructure

Mitigation strategies:
- network monitoring
- endpoint detection
- firewall rules
- device patching
- threat intelligence feeds
